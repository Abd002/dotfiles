Neovim Keys

Every key in your config, grouped the way the editor groups them. Space is the leader: press it and wait a moment, and a menu lists every group. In Neovim, SpaceK opens this sheet (search it with /), SpacesK fuzzy-searches it, and Space? shows the keys for the current file.
